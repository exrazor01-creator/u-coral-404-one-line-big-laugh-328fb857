## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **One whole funny line** — a complete joke, self-contained, that needs
   no setup or second line.
2. **A real turn** — a vacuum stuck under the couch sounds like a
   household mishap, then it comes out demanding a union: the appliance
   is not broken, it is organised.
3. **Tight** — 82 characters, every word earning its place, nothing left
   to trim.
4. **Fresh** — the absurdly specific detail (three days, under the
   couch) plus a labour dispute from a cleaning robot is not a recycled
   template or a stock pun.
5. **Stays in the head** — "demanding a union" is the kind of ending
   someone repeats to a friend the same day.
